About this record

This record shows how the Hampyeong Industrial Cooperative of Record 008 became a joint-stock company in 1925.

Summary translation (the article's account)

Hampyeong Heungsan Co. began as an industrial cooperative founded in 1920 (Taishō 9) at the initiative of Lee Jae-hyeok, Jang Ji-mun and others. Its results were good enough to pay dividends, and it was reorganized as a joint-stock company with capital of 80,000 won, completing registration on 16 October.

Its main business was rice milling and cotton ginning, along with other farm produce. Officers included Lee Jae-hyeok as president and managing director, and Lee Jae-seung as adviser (相談役).

What the record confirms

According to the Maeil Sinbo, the cooperative was founded in 1920 at the initiative of Lee Jae-hyeok and others, and completed registration as a joint-stock company with capital of 80,000 won on 16 October 1925.

The commercial registration page in Official Gazette No. 3988 (published 4 Dec. 1925) gives the company's date of establishment as 3 October 1925 and its date of registration as 12 October 1925.

The Maeil Sinbo reported registration as "completed on 16 October," which differs from the gazette's registration date (12 October). These two sources alone do not show whether the newspaper referred to a different procedural step or simply reported a different date. This entry gives the gazette's dates as the official registration record and the newspaper's date as contemporary reporting.

According to the Maeil Sinbo, Lee Jae-hyeok was president and managing director and Lee Jae-seung was an adviser.

The company's registration appears in the Government-General's Official Gazette No. 3988 (published 4 Dec. 1925). The registered officers listed there do not include advisers, so Lee Jae-seung's adviser title is confirmed only by the newspaper (series Part 6).
