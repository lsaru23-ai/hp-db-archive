1891 Seungjeongwon Ilgi — A Posthumous Title for Filial Piety: Lee Gyeong-heon

Historical Records & Media Coverage · record 024

“Historical Records & Media Coverage” presents period newspapers, official gazettes, documents, institutional records and press coverage related to the Lee Dong-beom family of Hampyeong, together with their original text (or, where a source is hard to read, the parts that can be read).

Each entry separates what the record confirms from what it cannot confirm on its own.

Category [Primary source] Seungjeongwon Ilgi

Record date Gojong 27/12/23 (lunar) = 1 February 1891

Source NIKH Seungjeongwon Ilgi database; original images at Kyujanggak Institute, Seoul National University

Original Entry of Gojong 27/12/23 (page image via the "Kyujanggak original" link)

About this record

This is the record of a posthumous office granted to Lee Gyeong-heon (李警憲, 1825–1855), grandfather of Seok-am Lee Dong-beom. The Hampyeong Lee Clan Comprehensive Genealogy notes "gyogwan" (敎官), among other titles, under his name; this government record relates to that entry.

Original text (excerpt)

故學生李警憲, 贈童蒙敎官, 孝行卓異贈職事, 承傳。

Translation

The late Lee Gyeong-heon, haksaeng (a scholar without office), is posthumously appointed dongmong gyogwan (instructor of youth); the royal order to grant this posthumous office for his outstanding filial piety is hereby transmitted.

What the record confirms

In 1891 (12th lunar month of 1890) the court posthumously granted Lee Gyeong-heon the title of dongmong gyogwan, for "outstanding filial piety" (孝行卓異).

The record describes him as haksaeng, a term for a scholar without office; this record alone does not settle his whole life history.

What this record alone cannot confirm

Whether the genealogy's "gyogwan" refers only to this posthumous title

What his filial conduct consisted of

Who petitioned for the title (it came about 35 years after his death)

The basis for the genealogy's additional entry "jobong daebu" (朝奉大夫, a junior fourth-rank grade), or how it relates to the title of dongmong gyogwan
