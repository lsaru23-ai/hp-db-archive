The Lee Dong-beom Family of Hampyeong: A 128-Year Bond — English Edition

[EN] English edition · 한국어 아카이브

This is the English edition of a family history series written by a descendant of the Lee Dong-beom family of Hampyeong, South Jeolla Province. It traces the bond that began in 1898, when the young Kim Gu stayed for about two weeks at the house of "Jinsa Lee" in Hampyeong, and follows that family through the records that survive.
