[Back Through Time] ② Chun-gang Lee Jae-seung — The Eldest Son and His Brother Lee Jae-hyeok

0001①②③④⑤⑥⑦

Before we begin

The second detailed chronology is about my great-grandfather Chun-gang Lee Jae-seung (春崗 李載昇, 1890–1962). I told his story once in Part 6 of the earlier series, "Chun-gang Lee Jae-seung — The Eldest Son Who Carried On the Main Line." This time I set out those records year by year again, and note what each one proves and what it does not.

His younger brother Lee Jae-hyeok (李載爀, born 1893) appears alongside him in almost every record. They studied in the same house in 1898, took roles in the same organization in 1923, and visited Kim Gu together in 1946. So the second half of this part gathers the story of the two brothers.

Sources: [G] government and public records (including the family register) · [F] originals and objects held by the family · [Gen] genealogy · [N] newspapers of the time · [L] later compilations, memoirs and survey reports (including Baekbeom Ilji) · [O] oral family tradition

Baekbeom Ilji is the memoir written by Kim Gu himself, and it is the most direct record of his 1898 stay in Hampyeong and the 1946 reunion, written by the person who lived them. Because it was written years after the events, names and dates in it are read against other records.

2. What the records show

My great-grandfather's records fall into three strands.

The first is government records. His appointment as chambong of Sunneung in 1904 and his departure two days later are recorded in both the Official Gazette and the Biseowon Ilgi, so they are certain. The records do not explain why he was appointed at so young an age.

The second is newspapers. From 1923 to 1938 his name appeared several times in the press — as cooperative head, custodian, adviser, and as the subject of a profile. These articles confirm that he was a figure whose name was attached to an industrial cooperative, an education movement and the founding of a company in Hampyeong.

The third is later compilations and family tradition. The 1913 record of his school committee role and the story surrounding his daughter-in-law's withdrawal from school belong here.

3. What the records do not show

Service as township head: The 1938 article introduces him as a former head of Hampyeong township. We have not yet found a personnel record showing when he served. Not having found it does not mean he did not serve, and we are still comparing official and local-history sources.

The title of adviser: He appears as an adviser in the 1925 newspaper report, but the Gazette registration does not list an adviser separately. This title is confirmed only by the newspaper.

Gisan Farm tenant farmers' association: It is mentioned in the 1938 article, but his role and actual activities need to be confirmed from other sources.

1926 to 1936: Some records for this period are still under review.

The Tongdosa inscription: On the base of a stone pagoda in the grounds of Tongdosa temple in Yangsan, South Gyeongsang Province, there is an inscription read as 「李載昇」. Earlier family research records the inscription as 「李東範 子 載昇」 ("Jae-seung, son of Lee Dong-beom"). Because the father-and-son relationship between Lee Dong-beom and Lee Jae-seung is confirmed by the genealogy and the 1917 Maeil Sinbo, there is substantial ground to regard this inscription as related to Chun-gang Lee Jae-seung. In the photographs we currently hold, however, only the part 「李載昇」 can be read with reasonable clarity, so the whole inscription needs to be confirmed by comparing the 「李東範 子」 part on the stone itself or in other photographs.

Year of death: The family register gives 1962 and the genealogy 1959.

4. Two brothers, Lee Jae-seung and Lee Jae-hyeok

Lee Jae-hyeok was born in 1893, the second son of Lee Dong-beom, three years younger than his brother.

1898 · Young brothers studying in the house where Kim Gu stayed; the elder about eight, the younger about five

Source: [L] Baekbeom Ilji

1917 · The Maeil Sinbo article on their father introduces both sons, with photographs of the two brothers

Source: [N] → Series Part 4

1923 · In the Hampyeong branch of the People's University Movement, the younger was chairman of the executive committee and the elder custodian

Source: [N] → Archive Record 008

1925 · Both brothers' names appear in the report on Hampyeong Heungsan Co., Ltd.

Source: [N] → Archive Record 009

1946 · Visited Kim Gu in Seoul together

Source: [L] Baekbeom Ilji → Series Part 3

The elder carried on the main family line; the younger's name survives today in the "Lee Jae-hyeok House" in Hampyeong, which is designated a cultural heritage material of South Jeolla Province. A 2003 survey report states that the house had been known as "the house of Jinsa Lee (李進士家)," and that the present sarangchae (men's quarters) was built in 1917 and the gate quarters in 1929. [L] The relationship between the two houses is discussed in detail in Series Part 4.

The original text of Baekbeom Ilji writes the names of the two men who came in 1946 as 「在昇·在赫」. The family genealogy and the 1917 Maeil Sinbo print them as 「載昇·載爀」. Which spelling was the official form of the names at the time still needs to be confirmed separately.

Lee Jae-hyeok's year of death has not yet been confirmed for this series.

Closing

The two brothers who studied in the same house in 1898 put their names to the same organization in 1923, and in 1946 went together to see their old guest. Setting their records side by side, my great-grandfather's chronology turned out to have been the chronology of two people from the start.

The next part looks at the chronology of the two brothers' father, my great-great-grandfather Seok-am Lee Dong-beom.
