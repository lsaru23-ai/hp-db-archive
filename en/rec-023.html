1877 Seungjeongwon Ilgi — Relief Funds from Lee Hong-gyu, Former Owijang of Hampyeong

Historical Records & Media Coverage · record 023

“Historical Records & Media Coverage” presents period newspapers, official gazettes, documents, institutional records and press coverage related to the Lee Dong-beom family of Hampyeong, together with their original text (or, where a source is hard to read, the parts that can be read).

Each entry separates what the record confirms from what it cannot confirm on its own.

Category [Primary source] Seungjeongwon Ilgi (Royal Secretariat Diary)

Record date Gojong 14/8/21 (lunar) = 27 September 1877

Related entries Military-examination diploma (hongpae), 4th month of 1871 (held by the family) · Seungjeongwon Ilgi, Gojong 8 (1871) 9/13 and 9/21; Gojong 20 (1883) 5/3

Source National Institute of Korean History, Seungjeongwon Ilgi database; original images at Kyujanggak Institute, Seoul National University

Original Entry of Gojong 14/8/21 (page image via the "Kyujanggak original" link)

About this record

Lee Gye-song (李啓松), father of Seok-am Lee Dong-beom, is also recorded in the Hampyeong Lee Clan Comprehensive Genealogy under another name, Hong-gyu (弘奎). The Seungjeongwon Ilgi for 1877 lists "Lee Hong-gyu, former owijang of Hampyeong" (咸平前五衛將李弘奎) among those who contributed 1,500 nyang toward famine relief. The entry is a proposal by the Ministry of Personnel to reward voluntary contributors (願納人), based on the report and roll submitted by Yi Don-sang, Governor of Jeolla Province, on completing the relief.

The Hampyeong head family still holds the royal diploma (hongpae) recording Lee Hong-gyu's success in the military examination in 1871.

Original text (excerpt)

各邑願納人中 … 咸平前五衛將李弘奎, 金堤前五衛將金光栢, 各錢一千五百兩,
上項二人, 施以相當職調用。

Translation

Among the voluntary contributors of the counties … Lee Hong-gyu, former owijang of Hampyeong, and Kim Gwang-baek, former owijang of Gimje, each gave 1,500 nyang; it is proposed that these two be employed in suitable posts.

The military-examination diploma kept by the family

敎旨
閑良李弘奎武科乙科
第二人及第出身者
同治十年四月　日

"Royal decree. Hallyang Lee Hong-gyu has passed the military examination, second place in the second (eulgwa) class, and is admitted to official status. 4th month of the 10th year of Tongzhi (1871)." An accompanying title strip also reads "閑良李弘奎武科乙科第二人." Hallyang (閑良) denotes a candidate of military background who had not yet entered office. The genealogy entry for Lee Gye-song (1989 edition, vol. 8, p. 779; online genealogy printout, vol. 11, p. 1080) records "also named Hong-gyu (弘奎)" and a military cheomchu title in the sinmi year (1871), the same year as the diploma.

Other entries under the same name (Seungjeongwon Ilgi) — given his examination success (4th month of 1871) and the sequence of his career, these entries are likely to refer to the same person (an inference); each entry is still to be checked individually against the examination roster and is not yet confirmed

9/13/1871 (lunar; 26 Oct.): appointed owijang (李弘奎爲五衛將); in a later round, listed as sole nominee for cheomji jungchubusa (僉知單李弘奎)

9/21/1871 (lunar): owijang Lee Hong-gyu and others replaced on grounds of illness

1873–1874: repeated appointments as owijang and replacements on grounds of illness

5/3/1883 (lunar; 7 June): named on a list of additional owijang appointments (五衛將加設單)

What the record confirms

A government record shows that "Lee Hong-gyu, former owijang of Hampyeong" contributed 1,500 nyang to famine relief in 1877. On the original page image, the name, place and amount match the database transcription.

The Ministry of Personnel proposed employing him in a suitable post in recognition.

He had already served as owijang before 1877 ("former owijang").

Lee Hong-gyu passed the military examination in the 4th month of 1871, second place in the eulgwa class (diploma held by the family). The Seungjeongwon Ilgi records an owijang of the same name appointed in the 9th month of the same year, and the 1877 entry names "Lee Hong-gyu, former owijang of Hampyeong." That the examination and the owijang appointment belong to the same man's career is an inference from their timing.

What this record alone cannot confirm

Whether he was actually appointed to a post as proposed (the entry records only the proposal)

What 1,500 nyang amounted to at the time, or how it was paid

Whether the Lee Hong-gyu of the 1871, 1873 and 1883 entries is the Lee Hong-gyu of Hampyeong; those entries give no place of origin. The grounds for regarding them as likely to refer to the same person are the 1871 examination diploma followed in the same year by an owijang appointment (an inference from timing), the 1877 entry ("former owijang of Hampyeong"), the genealogy's "also named Hong-gyu" and its 1871 military cheomchu title, and the 1913 Joseon Sinsa Daedongbo entry for Lee Dong-beom giving his father as "李弘奎 (同中樞)" (Record 026).

The exact name of the 1871 examination, his overall rank, and the residence and father's name recorded in the examination roster. These will be added once the roster has been checked.

Whether the posts of owijang and cheomji jungchubusa involved actual duties or were largely honorary
