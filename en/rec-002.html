1899 Hwangseong Sinmun — Notice of a Lost Seal by "Former Chambong Lee Dong-beom"

Historical Records & Media Coverage · record 002

"Historical Records & Media Coverage" presents period newspapers, official gazettes, documents, institutional records and press coverage related to the Lee Dong-beom family of Hampyeong, together with their original text (or, where a source is hard to read, the parts that can be read).

Each entry separates what the record confirms from what it cannot confirm on its own.

Category [Primary source] Newspaper notice

Record date 23 August 1899, Hwangseong Sinmun, page 3

Event Night of the 2nd day of the 7th lunar month, 1899 (seal lost)

Source Hwangseong Sinmun, 23 August 1899, p. 3

Original Page image included in series Part 5; the newspaper can be viewed through the National Library of Korea newspaper archive

About this record

This is the earliest newspaper record found so far that names Lee Dong-beom.

It is a short notice published the year after Kim Koo's stay in Hampyeong.

Original text

廣告
前參奉李東範氏가陰七月初二日夜에陰陽刻ᄒᆞᆫ姓名章을見失ᄒᆞ얏ᄉᆞ니
該日븟터其圖章은施行치아니ᄒᆞᄋᆞᆸ
李東範 白

Reading corrected (Nov. 2026): by comparing letter forms within the same notice on the page (the horizontal stroke of 을 and the lower dot of ᄒᆞ), '施行치아니ᄒᆞᆷ' has been corrected to '施行치아니ᄒᆞᄋᆞᆸ'.

Translation

Notice. Mr. Lee Dong-beom, former chambong, lost his name seal, carved in both intaglio and relief, on the night of the 2nd day of the 7th lunar month.

From that day the seal is no longer valid. — Announced by Lee Dong-beom.

What the record confirms

In August 1899 Lee Dong-beom was referred to as a "former chambong" (前參奉).

This matches the 1897 gazette record of his appointment and resignation (Record 001).

The notice was placed in his own name, signed "李東範 白" (announced by Lee Dong-beom).

What this record alone cannot confirm

How or where the seal was lost

Any connection with Kim Koo's 1898 stay (the notice does not mention it)

It does not by itself prove that "Jinsa Lee" in Baekbeom Ilji was Lee Dong-beom.
