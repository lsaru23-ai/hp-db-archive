1897 Official Gazette — Appointment and Resignation at Jogyeongmyo

Historical Records & Media Coverage · record 001

"Historical Records & Media Coverage" presents period newspapers, official gazettes, documents, institutional records and press coverage related to the Lee Dong-beom family of Hampyeong, together with their original text (or, where a source is hard to read, the parts that can be read).

Each entry separates what the record confirms from what it cannot confirm on its own.

Category [Primary source] Official Gazette (Gwanbo), issued by the Joseon government before the proclamation of the Korean Empire

Record dates Appointed 28 Jun. 1897 (gazette 30 Jun.) · resigned 4 Jul. (gazette 6 Jul.)

Period covered Late June – early July 1897

Source National Institute of Korean History, Korean History Database, "Official Gazettes of Joseon and the Korean Empire" · original images: Kyujanggak Institute, Seoul National University

Original No. 676 · No. 681 · Seungjeongwon Ilgi (Royal Secretariat Diary), Gojong 34/5/29 and 6/5

About this record

This is the earliest record found so far of a government post held by Seok-am Lee Dong-beom. June–July 1897 predates the proclamation of the Korean Empire (12 October 1897), so this gazette was issued by the Joseon government (the database collection is titled "Official Gazettes of Joseon and the Korean Empire").

A 1917 Maeil Sinbo profile said he "began his official career as chambong (custodian) of Jogyeongmyo"; this gazette entry confirms it in a government record. Jogyeongmyo is the shrine in Jeonju dedicated to the progenitor of the Jeonju Yi lineage.

Original text (excerpt)

No. 676, Appointments (entries of 23, 27 and 28 June): 任肇慶廟參奉敍判任官八等 李東範
No. 681, Appointments (entries of 2–4 July): 肇慶廟參奉 李東範 依願免本官
任肇慶廟參奉李東範 (Seungjeongwon Ilgi, Gojong 34/5/29 lunar = 28 June 1897)
肇慶廟參奉李東範, 依免 (6/5 lunar = 4 July 1897)

Translation

No. 676: Lee Dong-beom is appointed chambong of Jogyeongmyo, ranked eighth grade of the panim (junior official) class.

No. 681: Lee Dong-beom, chambong of Jogyeongmyo, is relieved of his post at his own request.

What the record confirms

He was appointed chambong of Jogyeongmyo on 28 June 1897 and left the post at his own request on 4 July 1897. Converted from the lunar calendar, the dates in the Seungjeongwon Ilgi match the appointment dates in the Official Gazette.

This explains why a newspaper notice of 1899 calls him a "former chambong" (Record 002).

What this record alone cannot confirm

Whether he actually served at the shrine, or what his duties were

Why he resigned so soon

The year given in a 2003 survey report ("chambong in 1902"). The gazette shows 1897, and the report's year does not match other records.
