1913 Joseon Sinsa Daedongbo — Entries for Lee Dong-beom and His Son Lee Jae-seung

Historical Records & Media Coverage · record 026

“Historical Records & Media Coverage” presents period newspapers, official gazettes, documents, institutional records and press coverage related to the Lee Dong-beom family of Hampyeong, together with their original text (or, where a source is hard to read, the parts that can be read).

Each entry separates what the record confirms from what it cannot confirm on its own.

Category [Primary source] Biographical directory

Record date 1913 (Joseon Sinsa Daedongbo, "Comprehensive Register of Korean Gentlemen"; per the database's bibliographic data — original volume not yet examined)

Source NIKH Korean History Database, "Modern Biographical Materials" (data from the Joseon Sinsa Daedongbo)

Original Lee Dong-beom · Lee Jae-seung (database transcription; page images of the book not yet obtained)

About this record

A directory compiling the careers of local notables, published early in the colonial period. It contains separate entries for Lee Dong-beom and his eldest son, Lee Jae-seung. As with most such directories, the information was probably supplied by the subjects or their families.

Original (database entries, excerpt)

Lee Dong-beom (李東範): clan seat Hampyeong · born 13 Nov. 1869 · father 李弘奎(同中樞) · 縣監 號孝友堂 李慴 十七世孫 · education 漢文受學 · career 肇慶廟參奉 中樞院議官 正三品通政 · assessment 萬頃 殷栗郡守 作宰廉直 蔚有遺愛

Lee Jae-seung (李載昇): clan seat Hampyeong · born 9 Oct. 1890 · father 李東範(通政) · 縣監 號孝友堂 李慴 十八世孫 · education 漢文受學 · career … 咸平郡公立普通學校 學務委員

Translation

Lee Dong-beom: of the Hampyeong Lee clan, born 13 November 1869; father Lee Hong-gyu (dongjungchu); seventeenth-generation descendant of Lee Seup (pen name Hyowudang), a former county magistrate; educated in classical Chinese. Career: chambong of Jogyeongmyo, councillor of the Jungchuwon, senior third rank (tongjeong daebu). Assessment: "As magistrate of Mangyeong and Eunyul he governed with integrity and left many fond memories."

Lee Jae-seung: of the Hampyeong Lee clan, born 9 October 1890; father Lee Dong-beom (tongjeong); eighteenth-generation descendant of Lee Seup; educated in classical Chinese. School affairs commissioner (學務委員) of Hampyeong County Public Common School.

What the record confirms

Around 1913 Lee Dong-beom and Lee Jae-seung were both listed in a contemporary biographical directory.

Lee Dong-beom's listed career (Jogyeongmyo chambong, Jungchuwon councillor, senior third rank) broadly matches the Official Gazette (Record 001, Record 007).

His father is given as "李弘奎." Together with the genealogy's "Lee Gye-song, also named Hong-gyu," this is a contemporary record supporting that Lee Gye-song and Lee Hong-gyu were the same person (Record 023).

Lee Jae-seung served as a school affairs commissioner of Hampyeong County Public Common School.

What this record alone cannot confirm

The objectivity of the assessment "作宰廉直 蔚有遺愛." It is an evaluative phrase printed in the directory, not an independent official performance record; this entry reports only that the phrase appears.

The exact meaning of "同中樞." It appears to abbreviate dongji jungchubusa (junior second rank), but this is not confirmed. His gravestone and the genealogy record a cheomji jungchubusa (senior third rank) title; whether these reflect different periods needs checking.

The date and circumstances of the promotion to the senior third rank (the gazette record is still being checked)

How long Lee Jae-seung served as commissioner, or what he did
